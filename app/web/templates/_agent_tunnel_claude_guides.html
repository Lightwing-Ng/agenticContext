    Add the AgenticContext connector in Claude

    • Open Connectors

      In Claude, open Customize › Connectors, click +, then choose Add custom connector.

      Open Connectors
    • Fill in the custom connector

      Name it AgenticContext and paste the MCP URL. Open Advanced settings, paste the client ID and client secret from Step 2, then click Add.

    • Connect and approve the callback here

      Click Connect next to AgenticContext. Come back to this page, check that the callback is exactly https://claude.ai/api/mcp/auth_callback, and click Approve callback. Claude then shows the connector as connected.

    • Turn it on in a chat

      In a new chat, click + at the lower left, point to Connectors, and switch AgenticContext on. Claude asks before each project action unless you allow it.

      Open Claude
